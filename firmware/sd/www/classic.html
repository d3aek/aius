Input channels

Per-channel trim runs before the downmix. Up to two EQ sections per channel; none removes the section from the config, bypass keeps a present but inactive one. A row's reset returns that section to the factory default.

Output EQ

Downmix matrix

FL and FR cross at a fixed 1.0: the matrix has no field for them

Drag a handle with mouse or touch to set its gain; focus the diagram and use the wheel or arrow keys for fine adjustment. Double-click resets a handle, and the crossover label opens its field below.

Matrix values

Centre and surround gains are shared by both stereo sides by design, and are linear multipliers rather than decibels. With the clip-free policy selected the device resolves headroom itself. Headroom and the clip-free policy describe the summing case: with layout-aware headroom on, the pad applies only to layouts that sum (any of centre, LFE or the surrounds), so mono and stereo pass-through run at source level; turn it off to pad every layout as before.
